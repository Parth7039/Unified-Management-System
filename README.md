# 🧾 Unified Management System

A powerful and all-in-one desktop solution built specifically for contractors to manage operations efficiently — from invoicing to inventory, employee management, and real-time dashboards.

---

## 📦 Overview

**Unified Management System** provides contractors with the tools they need to streamline business operations:

- 🧾 Create and customize tax invoices
- 📦 Manage inventory and stock
- 💰 Track sales and payments
- 👷 Manage employees
- 📊 Visualize performance through an interactive dashboard

Built using **Flutter**, this system is optimized for desktop environments and offers a clean, intuitive UI for quick access to critical business insights.

---

## ⚙️ Features

### 🧾 Invoice Management
- Generate professional tax invoices
- Customize with project-specific details
- Save and view invoice history

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Add, update, or remove items
- Track stock levels
- Alert on low stock

### 💰 Sales Management
- Record and view sales data
- Link sales to specific customers and projects
- Track payment status

### 👷 Employee Management
- Add contractor or full-time employees
- Assign roles, tasks, and responsibilities
- Maintain salary and attendance logs

### 📊 Dashboard Analytics
- Interactive pie charts and bar graphs
- Real-time business insights
- Summarized views of inventory, sales, and team activity

- **MySQL / SQLite** – Data persistence (based on your implementation)
